How to practice these

  1. Start at 70 BPM with the click on. Count "1-e-&-a" out loud — the syncopated snare hits in the sheet groove live on the "a" of 2 and the "e" of 3, and counting is the fastest way to lock them in.
  2. Loop one card until four clean bars in a row feel easy, then nudge the tempo up by 5. Resist jumping to 120 early — clean and slow always wins.
  3. Isolate lanes. If a pattern fights you, play only kick + snare first, then add the hi-hat back on top.
  4. Use auto-advance as a workout. Turn on "auto-advance every 8 bars" and let the app walk you through all ten patterns without stopping — great for endurance and quick switching.
  5. Make it yours. Tap cells to move a kick or add a ghost note. Hi-hat cells cycle closed → open → off; snare cells cycle accent → ghost → off.
