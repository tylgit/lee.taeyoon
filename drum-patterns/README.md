# Groove Lab — Drum Pattern Practice

Ten upbeat drum patterns built around the groove from the practice sheet.
Open **`index.html`** in a browser for the interactive version: it plays every
pattern with adjustable tempo, a metronome click, an auto-advance practice
mode, and editable grids so you can invent your own variations.

## How to read the grids

Each bar is 16 sixteenth-note steps, counted `1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a`.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `x`    | closed hi-hat |
| `O`    | open hi-hat |
| `o`    | snare hit |
| `·`    | ghost snare (very quiet) |
| `●`    | kick |

## 1. The Sheet Groove *(from the practice sheet — level 3)*

Open hi-hat on 1, kick on beat 1 and every "&", snare on 2 and 4 plus two
syncopated 16ths: the **"a" of 2** and the **"e" of 3**.

```
     1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a
HH:  O   x   x   x   x   x   x   x

```

## Practice plan

1. **Start at 70 BPM with the click on.** Count "1-e-&-a" out loud — the
   syncopated hits only lock in when you can say where they live.
2. **Loop one pattern until four clean bars in a row feel easy**, then raise
   the tempo by 5 BPM. Clean and slow beats fast and sloppy.
3. **Isolate lanes.** If a pattern fights you, play kick + snare alone first,
   then add the hi-hat back.
4. **Chain them.** In the interactive player, turn on auto-advance and play
   all ten patterns back to back for endurance and quick switching.
5. **Invent variations.** Move one kick, add one ghost note — one changed
   cell per week compounds fast.
